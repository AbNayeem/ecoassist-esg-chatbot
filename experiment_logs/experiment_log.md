# NotebookLM Experiment Log

## Scenario

Comparison of GRI 305 emissions disclosures and BHP greenhouse gas reporting and climate governance.

---

## Sources Used

1. GRI 305 Standard
2. BHP Climate Transition Action Plan
3. BHP Sustainability Report

---

## Questions Asked

### Question 1

What are the key differences between how GRI 305 approaches emissions disclosure and how BHP calculates and reports its GHG emissions?

### Question 2

What specific methane measurement methods does BHP use in Australia?

### Question 3

How does BHP calculate emissions on an adjusted basis?

### Question 4

How does BHP's market-based Scope 2 reporting affect its targets?

### Question 5

How does BHP's Climate Transition Action Plan address climate governance and risk management?

---

## Outputs Generated

- Chat responses
- Summary notes
- Mind map
- Audio overview (~20 minutes)
- Study guide

---

## Observations

NotebookLM produced source-grounded answers and generated useful study materials. The audio overview was useful for revision and understanding relationships between concepts. However, all outputs require human verification because AI-generated summaries may omit details or oversimplify complex information.

---

## Conclusion

NotebookLM is suitable for limited academic use and supports research, revision and ESG policy analysis, but users should validate outputs against the original sources.
